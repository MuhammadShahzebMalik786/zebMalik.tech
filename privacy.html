Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. We believe in strict data privacy, zero uploads for our free tools, and transparency.

1. The Short Version

Your files are yours. All of the image, PDF, and text tools on this website run locally in your browser using JavaScript. We do not upload, store, or process your files on our servers. When you click a tool, your browser handles everything.

2. Data We Collect

Because our tools operate entirely on your device, we collect very little data. However, for the website to function, we and our partners may collect the following information:

  • Analytics Data: We may use basic analytics to see which pages are popular and how users find us (e.g., browser type, referring page, general geographic region).
  • Contact Information: If you reach out to us via email or our contact forms for agency services, we keep your name, email address, and project details to communicate with you.
  • Cookies and Advertising: We may use third-party vendors, including Google, who use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites.

3. Google AdSense & DoubleClick Cookie

We may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings. (Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info).

4. How We Use Information

The limited information we do collect is used to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our free tools and paid agency services.
  • Respond to your comments, questions, and requests for quotes.
  • Monitor and analyze trends, usage, and activities in connection with our Services.
  • Serve relevant advertisements to support the free tools on our site.

5. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect the data we do handle (like your contact inquiries). Because our tools process your documents locally on your device, the security of those files relies on your own device and browser security.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at our contact page or via our provided email channels.
