Questions

Asked before every engagement.

Is what you're doing legal?

We collect publicly accessible data and respect each site's terms and rate limits. If a job needs data behind a login we have no right to use, or something a site's terms clearly forbid, we say so instead of taking the work. What you do with the data afterwards is yours to answer for — if it includes personal data, check your obligations under GDPR or your local equivalent before you use it.

What happens when the target site changes?

On the Operate plan, selector fixes are included — a site redesign is our problem, not a new invoice. On one-off projects we will fix a break free within 14 days of delivery.

How do you take payment?

Bank transfer, Wise or Payoneer. Half on start for projects above $300, the balance on delivery. Monthly plans are billed at the start of each month and can be cancelled any time.

Do we get the source code?

Yes. Scripts, workflows and documentation are handed over on request at no extra cost. We do not hold your automation hostage as a retention strategy.

What if the output isn't right?

You review a sample before the full run, which catches most misunderstandings while they're cheap. After delivery, revisions continue until the output matches the written scope.

Do we have to get on a call?

No. Scope, quote, samples and delivery all happen in writing. If you would rather talk it through, we can — but nothing about our process requires it.
