Boundaries

Things we will turn down.

Saying this upfront saves everyone a week.

Data we aren't authorised to take

Anything behind a login we have no right to use, or that a site's terms clearly prohibit. We will tell you before quoting, not after invoicing.

Unsolicited bulk messaging

We build outreach systems, but not spam operations. Volume without consent or a lawful basis is not a project we take.

Guaranteed trading returns

We build and test trading and research systems. We do not promise profit, and we won't write copy that implies it.
