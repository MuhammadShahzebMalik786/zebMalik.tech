Why bought lists underperform

Three failures, all avoidable.

Sold many times over

A list resold to a hundred buyers has been emailed a hundred times. The good contacts unsubscribed long before it reached you.

Unverified addresses

Bounce rates above 5% damage your sending domain reputation. One bad list can put your whole outbound programme in spam folders for months.

Wrong filters

Generic industry codes and headcount bands rarely match how you actually qualify. You end up paying to email people who were never buyers.

Compliance

Where we draw the line.

Is this GDPR compliant?

We build B2B lists from publicly available business information — company addresses and role-based contacts, not personal accounts. Under GDPR and the UK equivalent, B2B outreach usually relies on legitimate interest rather than consent, but that assessment is yours to make and document as the data controller. We will not scrape personal social profiles, and we exclude jurisdictions on request. If your use case needs consent-based data, we are not the right supplier and will say so.

Do you send the emails too?

We build the sending infrastructure and the sequences if you want them. We don't operate bulk unsolicited campaigns on your behalf — the sending reputation, and the legal responsibility, should sit with you.

What bounce rate should we expect?

Under 3% on verified lists. If a list we deliver bounces above 5%, we replace the failed contacts at no cost.

Can you match our existing CRM?

Yes — send a CSV export of what you already have and we dedupe against it before delivery, so you only pay for new contacts.
