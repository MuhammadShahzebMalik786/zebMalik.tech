We built zebMalik.tech to solve these exact problems. We brought three practical things together under one roof:

  • 1. Custom Engineering: Fixed-price automation, web scraping, and AI systems with full code handoff.

1. Custom Engineering & Business Automation

At our foundation, we are an engineering team based in Pakistan, working across US, UK, and European business hours. We help companies eliminate repetitive work and collect critical business data reliably.

We do not bill open-ended hourly rates. Every project has a written scope, a fixed agreed price, and complete code handoff upon delivery.

What We Build:

  • Data Extraction & Web Scraping: Custom web scrapers that gather public data into clean JSON or Excel spreadsheets, even from complex sites with anti-bot shields.
  • AI Workflows & n8n Automation: Connecting your apps (CRMs, Google Sheets, databases) using self-hosted n8n workflows and smart AI agents that work 24/7 without manual effort.
  • Backend APIs & Databases: Fast, reliable backends built in Python (FastAPI, Flask) and Node.js with PostgreSQL databases.
  • Data Operations & Cleansing: Cleaning messy datasets, removing duplicate records, and structuring raw data for your team.

Turnkey Solutions Ready to Deploy:

  • E-Commerce Price Monitoring: Tracks competitor prices and inventory shifts daily so you never miss a market change.
  • B2B Lead List Building: Accurate, verified contact lists of companies and decision-makers in your target industry.
  • AI Chatbots & RAG Systems: Smart customer support assistants trained strictly on your internal manuals and company documentation.

Want to test our data quality before hiring us? You can get a Free Sample Dataset or check our fixed tiers on our Pricing Page.

2. 18+ Free Tools That Respect Your Privacy

Most online file converters upload your confidential files (like tax records, bank statements, and family photos) to their cloud servers. That is a major privacy concern.

Every tool on the zebMalik.tech Tools Hub runs 100% locally in your own browser using WebAssembly and modern Canvas technology. Your files NEVER leave your device. No signup is required, and there are zero usage limits.

Document & PDF Suite:

  • Compress PDF: Reduce heavy PDF sizes so they easily fit into email attachments.
  • Merge PDF & Split PDF: Combine multiple documents into one, or pull out only the specific pages you need.
  • Extract Text & OCR: Pull raw text and scanned characters directly onto your clipboard.
  • Organize PDF Pages: Visually reorder, rotate, or delete individual pages in your document.
  • Watermark PDF & Add Page Numbers: Add security stamps and sequential numbering to documents.
  • PDF to Image & Rotate PDF: Turn PDF pages into clean images or fix sideways scans.

Image & Media Utilities:

  • Image Compressor: Shrink photo file sizes with real-time visual compression previews.
  • Image Converter: Quickly convert between modern WebP, PNG, JPG, and AVIF formats.
  • Image Metadata Remover: Scrub out hidden GPS coordinates, camera models, and timestamps before sharing photos online.
  • More Utilities: Image Resizer, Cropper, Watermarker, and Image to PDF converter.

Everyday Developer Tools:

  • JSON Formatter & Validator: Beautify, validate, and debug messy API payloads with instant syntax checking.
  • QR Code Generator: Generate crisp, high-resolution QR codes for websites, WiFi networks, and contact cards.
  • Password Generator: Create strong, random passwords right on your device with zero server transmission.
  • Word & Character Counter & Meme Generator.

On the zebMalik.tech Blog, every article is 100% free for anyone to read. To support the independent authors who contribute, we created a 40% Creator Revenue-Share Model.

How It Works for Writers:

  • 1. Write in the Studio: Use our visual editor at zebmalik.tech/write. You can format headings, add code blocks, and upload multiple photos. Every photo is automatically compressed to Google WebP format right in your browser for instant load speeds.
  • 2. Editorial Verification: Our team checks your draft to make sure it is original, helpful, and safe for readers.
  • 3. Earn 40% of Ad Revenue: Once published, your article serves standard Google ads. You receive 40% of the verified ad revenue generated by your readers.
  • 4. Live Analytics Dashboard: Log into your Author Dashboard anytime to see verified human reads and track your accrued earnings in real time.

4. Clear Rules, AdSense Safety, and Payouts

Because we partner directly with Google AdSense, keeping our ad network clean, honest, and fraud-free is our top priority.

Payout Details:

  • Low $5.00 Threshold: You don't have to wait to accumulate hundreds of dollars. Withdrawals start as soon as your balance reaches $5.00.
  • Monthly Clearing Cycle: Payouts clear once a month between the 25th and 28th, matching standard ad network accounting schedules.
  • Multiple Payment Methods: We support direct disbursements via Easypaisa, JazzCash, Raast (for writers in Pakistan), Wise, Payoneer, or direct USDT / Crypto transfer.

Strict Anti-Fraud Protection:

  • No Self-Clicking: Writers must never click on ads on their own articles.
  • Built-in Author Shield: We engineered an automatic protection system. When you view your own published article, all ads and view counters are automatically turned off for your session, keeping your AdSense account 100% safe.
  • Zero Bot Tolerance: Using traffic bots, click farms, or artificial engagement results in an instant permanent ban and forfeiture of balances. Only authentic human readers count.
  • Tax Transparency: Authors who cross $600 in cumulative annual earnings receive an automated notice to submit standard freelance verification for uninterrupted disbursements.
