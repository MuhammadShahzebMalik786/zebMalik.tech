Unicode Annex #29 & The Flesch-Kincaid Formula

Accurate word counting requires adherence to Unicode Standard Annex #29 (Text Segmentation) to handle hyphenated words, apostrophes, and multilingual scripts accurately.

Our tool computes the standard Flesch Reading Ease Score: 206.835 - 1.015 * (Total Words / Total Sentences) - 84.6 * (Total Syllables / Total Words). Higher scores (60–70) represent clear, engaging web copy, while scores below 40 reflect dense academic literature.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many pages is 500, 1000, or 2500 words?
At standard 12pt font with 1-inch margins, 500 words is 1 single-spaced page (or 2 double-spaced pages). 1,000 words is approximately 2 single-spaced pages (4 double-spaced), and 2,500 words is 5 single-spaced pages (10 double-spaced).
What is the difference between character count with and without spaces?
Character count with spaces tallies every alphabetical letter, digit, punctuation mark, and whitespace gap. Character count without spaces strictly counts alphanumeric characters and punctuation symbols, which is required for academic admissions and publishing limits.
How many words is a 5-minute speech?
An average speaker talks at approximately 125 to 150 words per minute. A 5-minute presentation requires approximately 650 to 750 words. A 10-minute speech requires approximately 1,300 to 1,500 words.
What is the ideal length for SEO titles and meta descriptions?
SEO meta titles should stay between 50 and 60 characters to avoid desktop and mobile SERP truncation. SEO meta descriptions should stay between 140 and 160 characters (including spaces) for optimal snippet display in Google search results.
How is reading time calculated?
Reading time is calculated using the benchmark of 200 to 220 words per minute (WPM) for silent reading. Dividing total word count by 200 gives estimated reading duration in minutes.
