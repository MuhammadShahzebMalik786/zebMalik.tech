QuickBite — Food Delivery & Cloud Kitchen App

Flutter • Node.js • Google Maps API • WebSockets • Stripe

View on GitHub Request Custom App

Project Overview

QuickBite is an end-to-end on-demand food delivery and restaurant management ecosystem featuring three synchronised applications: Customer App, Delivery Rider App, and Restaurant Kitchen Tablet Dashboard. Delivers fluid animations, instant menu customisation, sub-second live map tracking, and smart delivery routing.

Technology Stack

Flutter Dart Node.js / Express Google Maps SDK Socket.IO Stripe & Digital Wallets MongoDB / Redis

Key Features & Capabilities

  • Turnkey Live GPS Order Tracking: Real-time rider geolocation updates on interactive Google Maps with estimated arrival time (ETA).
  • Dynamic Restaurant Menus & Add-ons: Customizable dish options, combo builders, special dietary tags (Halal, Vegan, Gluten-free), and chef notes.
  • One-Click Reordering & Scheduled Delivery: Save favorite meals, schedule orders up to 7 days in advance, and apply promo codes.
  • Kitchen Order Ticket (KOT) Integration: Real-time sync between order placement, kitchen prep status, and rider pick-up notification.
  • Multiple Payment Gateways: Credit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Cash on Delivery (COD) with driver change calculation.
  • Rider Rating & Tip System: Two-way rating system and instant tipping for couriers.
