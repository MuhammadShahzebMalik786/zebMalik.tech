Project Overview

LuxeMart is an editorial-grade, mobile-first luxury fashion and apparel shopping app engineered to maximize conversion rates and visual storytelling. Features silky 60fps micro-interactions, swipeable editorial lookbooks, instant Algolia facet search, and seamless 1-click Apple Pay / Google Pay checkout.

Technology Stack

Flutter Dart Shopify GraphQL API Algolia InstantSearch Stripe SDK Lottie Animations Firebase Analytics

Key Features & Capabilities

  • Interactive Editorial Lookbook: "Shop the Look" hotspots where users tap garments on models to add items directly to bag.
  • Sub-100ms Instant Search & Filters: Real-time filtering by size, color palette, fabric, brand, price range, and in-stock availability.
  • AI Size & Fit Recommender: Personalized fit recommendations based on customer height, weight, and favorite brand comparisons.
  • One-Page Accelerated Checkout: Apple Pay, Google Pay, and credit card support with auto-address fill for zero-friction buying.
  • Real-Time Stock & Drop Alerts: Push notifications for low-stock warnings, price reductions, and exclusive seasonal collection drops.
  • Wishlist & Bag Cross-Device Sync: Real-time synchronization between mobile app and web storefront.
