Reed-Solomon Error Correction & Galois Field Math

QR codes rely on Reed-Solomon error correction over Galois Field GF(256). This mathematical redundancy allows scanners to reconstruct damaged, smudged, or partially obscured QR codes without data loss.

Selecting Level H (High - 30% recovery) is essential if you plan to overlay a custom logo in the center of the code or print on outdoor signage exposed to dirt and physical wear.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do these QR codes expire?
No. These are direct static QR codes. The encoded data is written directly into the black and white pixel matrix and will function forever without relying on external redirect servers.
Why is SVG format better than PNG for print?
SVG is an XML-based vector format. It can be scaled infinitely to billboard size with zero pixelation or blur.
