How we operate

Four commitments we'd rather be held to than described by.

01

Fixed prices, quoted first

You get a number and a date before work starts, against a scope written down in plain language. If the scope changes we re-quote. There is no hourly meter, so there is nothing to pad.

02

Written by default

Scoping, samples, quotes and delivery all happen in text. It is faster, it creates a record neither side can misremember, and it means we can work while you sleep. We'll take a call if you want one — nothing in our process requires it.

03

Your code, handed over

Scripts, workflows and documentation are yours on request. Some agencies keep the automation as leverage. We would rather you stayed because the work is good.

04

A straight no when it's a no

If a job is outside what we do well, or the data isn't ours to take, we say so before quoting. A fast rejection is worth more to you than a slow disappointment.

Where we are

Pakistan, working your hours.

We are based in Pakistan (UTC+5) and deliberately overlap with US, UK and EU working hours. In practice a brief sent at the end of your day is usually answered before the start of your next one.

It is also the honest reason our prices are what they are. The engineering is not cheaper; the cost base is.
