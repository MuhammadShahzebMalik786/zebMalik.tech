A practical first version

Start narrow, then make it smarter.

A focused knowledge base is easier to test and more useful than a chatbot that claims to answer everything.

STEP 01

Define the sources

Choose the documents, pages or product data the assistant is allowed to use.

STEP 02

Build retrieval

Chunk, index and retrieve relevant context with source references and sensible limits.

STEP 03

Design the chat

Give visitors a clear interface, useful empty states and a human handoff when needed.

STEP 04

Test and hand over

Review real questions, document known limits, and deliver the code and deployment notes.
