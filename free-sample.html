Questions

What people ask before sending one.

What's the catch?

There isn't one, but here is the honest reasoning: most people who see accurate data from their own target site go on to order the full job, and the ones who don't cost us an hour. It's cheaper than advertising and more convincing than a case study.

Will you keep emailing me?

No. You get the sample, the quote, and one follow-up a few days later. If you don't reply to that, we stop.

Can I ask for more than 100 rows?

Not free. A hundred rows is enough to judge field accuracy, formatting and completeness — which is the entire point. Beyond that it becomes the job itself.

What if the site is difficult?

Then the sample is more useful, not less. If a source has aggressive bot protection or a structure that makes extraction unreliable, we'd rather find that out on our own time and tell you honestly than discover it after you've paid.
